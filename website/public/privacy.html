Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Caremid provides care management software for UK care providers. This policy explains what personal information our website, manager portal, carer app and family portal handle, why, and what your rights are.

Our role: controller and processor

Most information in Caremid is entered by a care provider about the people they support and the staff they employ. For that information the care provider is the data controller and Caremid is a data processor acting on their instructions. If you receive care, or work for a care provider, and want to see, correct or delete your information, please contact that care provider directly. They decide how it is used, and we act on their instructions.

Caremid is the controller for the limited information we hold in our own right: the account and billing details of care providers who subscribe to us, and enquiries sent to us through this website.

What information the apps handle

  • Staff: name, email address, phone number, role, employment and compliance records such as DBS details, training and supervisions, rota, clock in and out times, and recorded hours.
  • People receiving care: name, address, date of birth, contact details, care and support plans, risk assessments, medication records (MAR), visit notes and support logs, incident and accident records, and, where the provider uses the feature, records of money handled on their behalf including photographs of receipts.
  • Family members: name and email address, where a provider invites a relative to the family portal.
  • Account and billing: the subscribing organisation’s contact and subscription details.

Care records include health information, a special category of personal data under UK GDPR. It is handled only to deliver and evidence care.

What we do not do

  • We do not track your location. The carer app records the time a visit starts and ends, not where you are.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics or tracking tools in the apps.
  • We do not sell personal information, or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use care records to train artificial intelligence models.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

As a processor, we handle care and staff records only to provide the service to the care provider, under our contract with them. Where we are the controller, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide and bill for the service.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and respond to enquiries.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records we are required to keep, such as for tax.

Who else is involved

We use a small number of suppliers to run the service. They act on our instructions and may not use the information for their own purposes:

  • Neon — database hosting
  • Render — application hosting
  • Vercel — hosting for the website and web apps
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage for uploaded documents and receipt photographs
  • Stripe — subscription payments from care providers. Stripe does not receive care records.
  • Brevo — sending service emails such as invitations and password resets

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect someone’s vital interests in an emergency.

Where information is stored

Our application servers and database are hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt). Uploaded files are stored with Cloudflare R2. Information may therefore be processed outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens we rely on UK adequacy regulations or appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long it is kept

Care and staff records are kept while the care provider’s account is active, and then in line with their instructions and their own retention obligations. Health and social care records are commonly kept for several years after care ends. When a provider ends their subscription we delete or return their data on request, subject to records we must keep by law. Backups are retained for a limited period and then overwritten.

How it is protected

All traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access is restricted by role, so staff see only the people and information their role requires, and providers can restrict access further by location. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in readable form. The database is backed up regularly, and access to production systems is limited to those who need it.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to your personal information, and to ask for it to be corrected, deleted or restricted, to object to its use, and to receive a copy in a portable form. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

If your information is held by a care provider using Caremid, please contact that provider first, as they are the controller. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request on to them.

Children’s information

The apps are used by care staff, who are adults. Where a care provider supports a young person, their records are handled on the same basis as any other person receiving care, under the provider’s instructions.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change, and will update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will tell subscribing providers directly.
